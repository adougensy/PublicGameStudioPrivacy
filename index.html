1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account linking (optional). If you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook to back up your progress, we receive a unique account identifier and, where the platform provides it, your display name and email address. Signing in is entirely optional; the Game is fully playable without an account.
  • Purchase information. When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple or Google. We receive a purchase receipt and product identifier for validation. We never receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or billing address.
  • Support correspondence. If you contact us, we keep the content of your message and your email address so that we can reply.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, screen resolution, language and country setting, time zone, network connection type, and mobile carrier.
  • Identifiers: advertising identifiers (Apple IDFA, Google Advertising ID), vendor/app-scoped identifiers (Apple IDFV), and a randomly generated in-game player ID.
  • IP address, used to determine approximate location at country or region level, to serve relevant content and to prevent fraud. We do not collect GPS or precise location.
  • Gameplay data: level progress, scores, in-game currency and item balances, boosters used, session start and end times, session length, and feature usage.
  • Advertising data: which ads were requested, shown, clicked, or completed, and rewards granted.
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports, error logs, frame-rate and performance metrics.
  • Attribution data: install source, referral and campaign parameters, and deep-link parameters used to measure the effectiveness of our marketing.

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your real name, postal address, phone number, government ID, precise GPS location, contacts, calendar, photos, health data, microphone audio, or camera images. The Game does not request these permissions.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Game, save your progress, and restore it across devices.
  • To deliver and validate in-app purchases and to prevent fraudulent transactions.
  • To display advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted by you and by applicable law.
  • To analyze aggregate gameplay data so we can balance difficulty, fix bugs, and improve features.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not use your information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Game, save progress, and deliver purchases.
  • Consent — for personalized advertising and related tracking. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 5).
  • Legitimate interests — for security, fraud prevention, aggregate analytics, and improving the Game, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data by law.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The Game is free to play and supported by advertising, including rewarded video and interstitial ads. Our advertising partners may use identifiers and interaction data to select which ads to show and to measure their performance.

Consent and opt-out

  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency). On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before accessing the IDFA for tracking. If you decline, we do not use the IDFA and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or limit ad tracking entirely in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads, and opt out of ad personalization there.
  • EEA / UK / Switzerland. Where required, we present a consent dialog on first launch (Google UMP / AppLovin consent flow) that lets you accept or refuse personalized advertising. You can withdraw or change your choice from the privacy option inside the Game's settings screen where available, or by contacting us.

Declining tracking does not remove ads — it means the ads you see are less relevant to you.

7. How We Share Information

We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With service providers listed in Section 6, strictly for the purposes described in this Policy.
  • For legal reasons — when required by law, court order, or a valid governmental request, or to investigate fraud, security incidents, or violations of our terms.
  • In a business transfer — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred, subject to this Policy.
  • With your consent — for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Game progress tied to a linked account is kept while the account remains active, and is deleted within 90 days of a verified deletion request.
  • Analytics and advertising event data are retained in aggregated or pseudonymized form, typically for no more than 26 months.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs are typically retained for up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records are retained as long as required by tax and accounting law.

Game data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

9. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and limiting access to personnel who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable minimum age in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

Where required, we apply age-appropriate settings and serve only non-personalized, contextual advertising to users identified as children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in Section 14 and we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

We and our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States and countries in Asia. Where data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

12. Your Privacy Rights

12.1 Everyone

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, and you may object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email us at the address in Section 14 and include your in-game player ID (visible in the Game's settings screen) so that we can locate your data.

12.2 EEA / UK (GDPR)

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12.3 California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used and shared, the right to delete it, the right to correct it, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise the opt-out, decline tracking when prompted (see Section 5) or contact us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

12.4 Deleting your data

You can delete all locally stored game data by uninstalling the Game. To request deletion of server-side data associated with a linked account, email us with your player ID; we will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and no later than 90 days.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will provide a notice inside the Game. Your continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 您主动提供的信息

  • 账号绑定（可选）。若您选择使用 Apple、Google 或 Facebook 登录以备份游戏进度，我们会获得一个唯一账号标识；在平台提供的情况下，还会获得您的昵称与电子邮箱。登录完全可选，不登录亦可完整游玩。

  • 客服往来信息。您联系我们时，我们会保留您的邮件内容与邮箱地址，以便回复。

1.2 自动收集的信息

  • 设备与技术信息：设备型号、操作系统及版本、屏幕分辨率、语言与国家／地区设置、时区、网络连接类型、移动运营商。
  • 标识符：广告标识符（Apple IDFA、Google 广告 ID）、设备／应用范围标识符（Apple IDFV），以及随机生成的游戏内玩家 ID。
  • IP 地址：用于判断国家或地区级别的大致位置、投放相应内容以及防范欺诈。我们不收集 GPS 或精确位置。
  • 游戏数据：关卡进度、分数、游戏内货币与道具余额、道具使用情况、会话起止时间与时长、功能使用情况。
  • 广告数据：广告的请求、展示、点击、完成情况以及发放的奖励。
  • 诊断数据：崩溃报告、错误日志、帧率与性能指标。
  • 归因数据：安装来源、推广活动参数与深度链接参数，用于衡量市场投放效果。

2. 我们不收集的信息

我们不会收集您的真实姓名、通信地址、电话号码、身份证件、GPS 精确位置、通讯录、日历、相册、健康数据、麦克风录音或摄像头影像。本游戏不会申请上述权限。
